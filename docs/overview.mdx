## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="User Speaks or Acts">
    The user talks or performs an action in front of the camera.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hawkeye-1 Recognises">
    Our vision model processes real-time video to detect actions, gestures, and context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="LLM Reasons">
    The agent's language model generates a contextually accurate, knowledge-grounded response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Huma-2 Renders">
    Our avatar renderer animates a lifelike response in real time, synced to voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agent Responds">
    The user receives a face-to-face response in under one second.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Foundation Models

TruGen is built on two proprietary models working in tandem:

| Model | Role | What It Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Huma-2** | Avatar Renderer | Generates expressive, lifelike avatar video synced to voice in real time |
| **Hawkeye-1** | Vision Model | Recognises real-world user actions, gestures, and context from live video |
