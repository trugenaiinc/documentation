Memory allows AI Teammates to save key details from one conversation and recall them in the next. Combined with **Custom Instructions**, you control exactly what gets remembered and how much detail is retained.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Developer Platform">
    Go to [app.trugen.ai](https://app.trugen.ai).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or open an agent">
    Click **Create New Agent** or open an existing one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Memory">
    Open the **Memory** tab and enable the **Custom Instruction** toggle. Write an instruction specifying what details the agent should save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a conversation">
    Talk to the agent. It will save memory entries based on your instruction and recall them in future conversations with the same user.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How Memory Works

When a conversation starts, the agent loads the user's previous chat history and selects the most relevant context to bring into the current session. As the conversation progresses, new details are stored — making them available the next time that user returns.

<Note>Review your privacy settings and ensure user consent is in place before storing sensitive personal details.</Note>

## Custom Instruction Examples

Custom Instructions tell the agent what categories of information to capture. Below are two ready-to-use examples.

### Sales Agent

```
1. Personal Profile
   - Capture full name, preferred name, date of birth or age, relationship status, family members and pets.
   - Record context and source ("User mentioned on 2024-05-10") and update whenever changes occur.

2. Life Events & Memories
   - Log notable experiences — vacations, celebrations, milestones — with date, location, participants, and significance.
   - Tag memories by theme (e.g., travel, accomplishment) and mark emotional valence (positive/negative/neutral).

3. Emotional & Mental Wellbeing
   - Track expressed moods, feelings, stressors, and sources of joy or concern.
   - Note changes over time to identify patterns.

4. Preferences & Interests
   - Store likes/dislikes across food, music, movies, hobbies, travel, and routines.
   - Include specificity (e.g., "Prefers jazz over classical", "Loves Thai cuisine — especially pad Thai").

5. Goals, Plans & Tasks
   - Record short- and long-term objectives, project steps, resolutions, and scheduled events with deadlines.
   - Track progress updates and flag overdue tasks.

6. Social Network & Communication
   - Note close friends, mentors, and colleagues with their relationship context and preferred contact methods.

7. Feedback & Reflections
   - Store evaluations of recent experiences, lessons learned, and aspirations for growth.

8. Health & Wellness Routines
   - Log exercise habits, dietary preferences, sleep patterns, and relaxation practices in a personal (non-clinical) context.

Implementation Guidelines:
- Timestamp every entry and note the context in which information was shared.
- Update or archive entries as details evolve; avoid duplication by merging overlapping data.
- Enable retrieval by keyword, category, date range, and emotional tag.
```

### Educational Mentor

```
Types of Information to Remember:

1. Personal & Academic Profile
   - User's name, institution, grade or year, major, enrolled courses.

2. Academic Goals & Aspirations
   - Target GPA, study-abroad plans, scholarship or internship applications, career objectives.

3. Assignments, Exams & Deadlines
   - Upcoming homework, projects, tests, exam dates, and study schedules.

4. Learning Preferences & Styles
   - Preferred media (visual, auditory, kinesthetic), class format, study environment, group vs. solo learning.

5. Extracurricular & Research Activities
   - Clubs, sports, competitions, volunteer work, and research projects.

6. Challenges & Support Needs
   - Difficult subjects, requests for tutoring or accommodations, time-management issues.

7. Achievements & Progress
   - Awards, certifications, completed courses, skills acquired, milestones reached.

8. Feedback & Motivation Factors
   - Opinions on classes or instructors, preferred teaching materials, motivational triggers or obstacles.

Memory Depth Guidance: Retain detailed context for the most recent three interactions; summarise older details as needed to preserve relevance and continuity.
```
