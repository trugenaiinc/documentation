The quality of a custom avatar depends almost entirely on the source image. Follow these guidelines to get lifelike results that render cleanly across expressions, angles, and lighting.

## Technical Specifications

| Requirement | Recommended |
| --- | --- |
| **Resolution** | 1024 × 1024 or higher |
| **Format** | JPG, PNG, WebP |
| **File size** | ≤ 10 MB |
| **Aspect ratio** | Square (1:1) or portrait (3:4, 4:5) |
| **Color space** | sRGB |

## Composition

<Steps>
  <Step title="Front-facing pose">
    The subject should look directly into the camera. Small head tilts are okay; profile shots are not.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Neutral or gentle expression">
    A closed-mouth smile or relaxed neutral expression works best. Avoid open-mouth laughs, exaggerated expressions, or eyes closed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Head and shoulders visible">
    Frame the subject from the mid-chest up. Leave a small margin above the head — don't crop the top of the head.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Uncluttered background">
    Use a plain, evenly-lit background. Avoid busy patterns, other faces, or high-contrast objects behind the subject.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Lighting

- **Even, diffuse light** across the face. Soft natural light near a window is ideal.
- **Avoid hard shadows** on one side of the face, from a hat brim, or under the eyes.
- **No direct back-lighting** — the subject should be brighter than the background.
- **No colored gels** or dramatic lighting effects.

## Face and Features

- **Face fully visible** — no sunglasses, no face masks, no obstructions.
- **Standard glasses are okay** but reflective glare on the lenses can cause artifacts.
- **Hair should not cover the eyes or eyebrows.**
- **Facial hair is fine** — beards, mustaches, stubble all render well.
- **Makeup is fine** — extreme theatrical makeup may reduce realism.

## Camera and Quality

- **Sharp focus on the eyes.** Blur anywhere on the face will carry into the avatar.
- **No heavy filters or beauty smoothing** — over-processed skin loses the texture Huma-3 needs to render naturally.
- **Avoid heavy compression artifacts** — export at high quality if resaving.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

<Warning>
- Group photos or multiple faces in the frame
- Selfies with distortion from ultra-wide phone lenses (stand back and zoom in instead)
- Cartoon avatars, AI-generated portraits, or heavily stylized illustrations (unless that's the intent)
- Old low-resolution photos scaled up
- Images with watermarks or logos over the face
</Warning>

## Consent and Rights

Only upload photos you have the legal right to use.

- For your own face, no additional permission is needed.
- For someone else's face, obtain their **explicit written consent** before creating an avatar.
- Do not create avatars of public figures, celebrities, or minors without proper authorization.

<Note>Uploading a photo of another person without their consent may violate privacy laws in your jurisdiction and is against TruGen's terms of service.</Note>
